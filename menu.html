Additional Menu Options

Japanese Cuisine

Experience authentic Japanese flavors with our extensive à la carte menu featuring fresh sushi rolls, sashimi, and specialty rolls. From classic California rolls to our signature Dragon Roll and House Roll, our skilled chefs prepare each dish with traditional techniques and the freshest ingredients. Perfect for those who prefer individual portions and want to explore the art of Japanese cuisine.

Chinese Menu

Indulge in our comprehensive Chinese menu offering over 100 dishes including appetizers, soups, fried rice, lo mein, and chef's specials. From traditional favorites like General Tso's Chicken and Sweet & Sour dishes to our popular lunch specials, we serve authentic Chinese cuisine prepared with fresh ingredients and authentic flavors. Available for dine-in, takeout, and delivery.
